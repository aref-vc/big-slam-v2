# Big 4 Grand Slam Tennis Tournaments - Sponsorship Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive, single-page HTML dashboard providing comprehensive sponsorship analytics for the four Grand Slam tennis tournaments: Wimbledon, US Open, French Open, and Australian Open. Built with Chart.js and featuring the elegant Midnight Flame color palette with Berkeley Mono typography.

## Live Demo

Open `dashboard.html` in your browser to view the complete analytics dashboard.

## Features

### 9 Comprehensive Sections

1. **Header & Key Metrics**
   - Total combined revenue: $1.82B
   - 114 total sponsors across all tournaments
   - $430M in total sponsorship revenue
   - 7 cross-tournament sponsors

2. **Revenue Analysis**
   - Total revenue comparison across tournaments
   - Revenue breakdown by source (broadcast, sponsorship, ticketing)
   - Sponsorship vs total revenue analysis

3. **Sponsorship Analysis**
   - Sponsorship revenue by tournament
   - Number of sponsors comparison
   - Top sponsor categories by tournament (grouped bar chart)
   - Average revenue per sponsor

4. **Financial Performance**
   - Profit margin comparison
   - Total prize money by tournament
   - Broadcast rights value
   - Revenue per attendee metrics

5. **Attendance & Engagement**
   - Tournament attendance 2024
   - Attendance per sponsor ratio

6. **Cross-Tournament Sponsors**
   - Brands present at multiple Grand Slams
   - Sponsor category distribution by tournament

7. **Top Sponsorship Deals**
   - Largest individual sponsorship deals
   - Top categories by total deal value

8. **Partnership Longevity**
   - Longest-running sponsorship partnerships
   - Average partnership duration by tournament

9. **Strategic Positioning Matrix**
   - Sponsors vs revenue per sponsor (scatter plot)
   - Revenue vs attendance with profit margin bubble size

10. **Detailed Tournament Comparisons**
    - Comprehensive comparison table with all key metrics

## Key Insights

### Tournament Strategies

**Wimbledon**
- Premium exclusivity model: 17 sponsors, $10M average per sponsor
- Highest broadcast rights value: $253M
- Longest partnership average: 35 years
- Heritage focus with Slazenger partnership since 1902 (122 years)

**US Open**
- Commercial leadership: 49% profit margin
- Highest total revenue: $534M
- Strong banking/finance sector presence (4 sponsors)
- Technology innovation with IBM partnership

**French Open**
- Balanced European model: 22 sponsors
- Strong beverage category (18% of sponsors)
- BNP Paribas partnership since 1973 (51 years)
- Clay court uniqueness

**Australian Open**
- Volume strategy: 50+ sponsors
- Highest attendance: 1.11M visitors
- Lowest revenue per sponsor: $1.4M
- Aggressive Asia-Pacific market focus

### Cross-Tournament Insights
- Only 2 brands sponsor all 4 Grand Slams: Rolex and Emirates
- Banking/Finance dominates top deals (44% of value)
- Average sponsorship deal value: $3.8M
- Combined economic impact: $1.82B+
